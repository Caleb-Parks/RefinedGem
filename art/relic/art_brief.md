# Refined Gem Relic — Art Brief

## Relic identity
- **Name:** Refined Gem
- **Rarity:** Ancient (Neow blessing)
- **Effect:** Replaces card pool with a custom curated pool from the Compendium
- **Flavor:** "Perfection, bottled."

## Technical specs
- **Size:** 200×200 px, 1:1 square PNG
- **Files:** color icon + separate outline/silhouette layer

## StS2 relic style (shared across all variations)
- Hand-drawn digital game icon in Slay the Spire 2 relic art style
- Crisp linework, saturated color, less painterly than StS1
- Single centered subject with strong readable silhouette at thumbnail size
- **Transparent background** (isolated subject, no vignette/backdrop)
- Multicolored **opalescent / iridescent** gem with pearlescent rainbow shifts
- Playful but slightly ominous Mega Crit tone
- No text, no UI frame, no photorealism
